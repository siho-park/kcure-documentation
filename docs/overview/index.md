# Program and data

K-CURE is a portal with several data products. The cancer registry that most registry studies need is part of the Cancer Public Library, which is the focus of this site.

- [The K-CURE project](project.md) describes who runs the project and the legal basis for releasing pseudonymized cancer data.
- [What the portal releases](products.md) lists the products and the three databases in the Cancer Public Library.
- [Applying for data](access.md) covers eligibility, documents, the review timetable, and fees.
- [Using the data](using.md) covers where each database is analyzed, the period of use, the safe-use centers, and export review.
