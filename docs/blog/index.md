# Research notes

These posts cover the practical side of the K-CURE data, from merging the tables to the caveats that the variable lists leave to the dictionary. They are written for researchers who are new to the data, and each post can be read on its own.

Researchers who have worked with the data are welcome to share what they have learned, and a post can be as short as a single pitfall. Send a draft in any format to [sihopark@kdischool.ac.kr](mailto:sihopark@kdischool.ac.kr?subject=K-CURE%20Data%20Documentation%20post), as described on the [contributing page](../about/contributing.md#writing-a-post).
