# Claim statement (T200)

`T200` (명세서 일반내역) is a table in the [Cancer Public Library](index.md). One row is one reviewed health-insurance claim, from 2007 through 2024, for people on [`RGST`](rgst.md). It is the hub of the claims: [`T300`](t300.md), [`T400`](t400.md), and [`T530`](t530.md) add the lines of the claim and join to it on `MID` (명세서조인키).

The table has three groups of items:

- the patient, including age and insurance type;
- the claim, including the start and end of care, inpatient or outpatient, the primary and secondary diagnoses, and the reviewed cost;
- the provider, as a replacement provider number.

Main and secondary diagnosis codes follow the same masking as [`T400`](t400.md). The variables are listed, each with its own page, in the [variable list](../../variables/public-library/t200.md).
