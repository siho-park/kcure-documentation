# Diagnoses on the claim (T400)

`T400` (상병내역) is a table in the [Cancer Public Library](index.md). One row is one diagnosis on a claim, primary or secondary, with the specialty. The years are 2007–2024, for people on [`RGST`](rgst.md) with a reviewed claim. The row joins to its claim on [`T200`](t200.md) on `MID` (명세서조인키).

These diagnosis codes are billing codes, stored up to four characters. Sensitive diagnoses are masked:

- Mental illness is reduced to its first character followed by asterisks, so `F00` becomes `F**`. A three-character code requires a research-use plan and committee approval.
- Rare diseases and sexually transmitted infections are masked entirely.
- Claims for HIV, miscarriage, abuse, and COVID-19 are removed.

The variables are listed, each with its own page, in the [variable list](../../variables/public-library/t400.md).
