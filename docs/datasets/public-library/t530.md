# Outpatient prescriptions (T530)

`T530` (원외처방내역) is a table in the [Cancer Public Library](index.md). One row is one drug prescribed to be dispensed outside the institution. Drugs given inside the institution are on [`T300`](t300.md). The years are 2007–2024, for people on [`RGST`](rgst.md) with a reviewed claim. The row joins to its claim on [`T200`](t200.md) on `MID` (명세서조인키).

The ingredient code (`GNL_CD`) is shortened. A research-use plan that covers dose, route, or formulation releases all eight characters. Cost variables also require a research-use plan.

`DY1_MDCT_QTY` (1일투약량) is the number of doses per day. For an ointment, for example, the dose per day and the days of use are both recorded as 1.

The variables are listed, each with its own page, in the [variable list](../../variables/public-library/t530.md).
