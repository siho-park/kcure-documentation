# Services (T300)

`T300` (진료내역) is a table in the [Cancer Public Library](index.md). One row is one service line on a claim: a procedure, a treatment material, or a drug given inside the institution. The years are 2007–2024, for people on [`RGST`](rgst.md) with a reviewed claim. The row joins to its claim on [`T200`](t200.md) on `MID` (명세서조인키).

Procedure codes are released in full, and material codes are shortened to the first five characters. A full material code and the cost variables require a research-use plan.

`DY1_MDCT_QTY` (1일투약량) is the number of doses per day, and the amount per dose is a separate variable. Total use is the amount per dose times the doses per day times the number of days.

The variables are listed, each with its own page, in the [variable list](../../variables/public-library/t300.md).
