# Checkup questionnaire, 2018–2024 (G1Q_1824)

`G1Q_1824` (일반건강검진 문진) is a table in the [Cancer Public Library](index.md). One row is the questionnaire answered at one general health examination in 2018–2024. The form changed over time, so the other years are in [`G1Q_0708`](g1q-0708.md) for 2007–2008 and [`G1Q_0917`](g1q-0917.md) for 2009–2017.

The questionnaire covers disease history and treatment, family history, hepatitis B, smoking, e-cigarettes and heated tobacco, drinking by type of drink, and physical activity. The row joins to the rest of the library on `SN_KEY`, and to the measured results on [`G1E`](g1e.md) on `SN_KEY` and the examination year `EXMD_BZ_YYYY`.

The people are those on [`RGST`](rgst.md) with a general health examination record from 2007 through 2024. Using any checkup table requires a research-use plan.

The variables are listed, each with its own page, in the [variable list](../../variables/public-library/g1q_1824.md).
