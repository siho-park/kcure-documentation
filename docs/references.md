## Data resource profile

Choi DW, Guk MY, Kim HR, Ryu KS, Kong HJ, Cha HS, Kim HJ, Chae H, Jeon YS, Kim H, Jung J, Im JS, Choi KS. Data Resource Profile: The Cancer Public Library Database in South Korea. *Cancer Research and Treatment*. 2024;56(4):1014-1026. [doi:10.4143/crt.2024.207](https://doi.org/10.4143/crt.2024.207)

This is the published description of the Cancer Public Library, written for its first release. The current years are on [sources and versions](datasets/sources.md).

## Related documentation

[NHIS Data Documentation](https://siho-park.github.io/nhis-documentation/) covers the National Health Insurance Service research databases. Those databases share claims, eligibility, and screening concepts with the public library, and they are a different extract.
